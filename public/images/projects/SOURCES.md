## Processing

All images retain their original aspect ratio and colours, without cropping or fabricated UI. Converted to WebP at quality 90: full images at up to 2000px tall, thumbnails at up to 720px tall. The numbered files preserve source ordering; `-thumb` versions are previews of the corresponding full images.
